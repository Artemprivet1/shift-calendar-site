Shift Calendar: My Work Schedule

Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Application: Shift Calendar: My Work Schedule
Updated: August 2026
Developer: Artem Kudrin (dastlap@gmail.com)

1. General Provisions

The "Shift Calendar" app does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Local Data Storage

All work shift schedules, notes, and events are stored 100% locally on your device using Jetpack DataStore.

3. Advertising and Identifiers

The application integrates the Appodeal mediation platform (Appodeal Inc., BidMachine) to display ads. The SDK may collect anonymous technical data (Advertising ID, coarse IP address). Privacy Policy: Appodeal Privacy Policy.

4. Data Management

To completely delete all data, simply uninstall the application from your device.

5. Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

• The application is provided "AS IS" for personal scheduling purposes only.

• The developer is not liable for missed shifts or lost wages due to alarm failures caused by OS battery optimization (Doze mode).

• Shift calculations and statistics are for reference only.

6. Contact Us

Developer: dastlap@gmail.com
