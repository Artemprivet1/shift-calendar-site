1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Shift Calendar" application handles your information. We respect your privacy: the application does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your full name, phone number, or personal contacts.

2. Local Data Storage

All your work shift schedules, custom patterns, notes, and personal calendar events are stored 100% locally on your device using Jetpack DataStore. The developer has no access to your schedule or personal calendar entries.

3. Advertising and Identifiers

To display advertisements and support app monetization, the application integrates the Appodeal mediation platform (Appodeal Inc., BidMachine). The ad SDK may collect anonymous technical data, including:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / Huawei OAID);
  • Device IP address (used for approximate coarse geo-targeting to display relevant local ads);
  • Device specifications, operating system version, and ad view/click performance metrics.
For more information regarding third-party ad data processing, please review: Appodeal Privacy Policy.
